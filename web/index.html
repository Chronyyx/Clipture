From the play to your friend’s screen.

  1. Before

    Play like you always do

    Clipture keeps a rolling buffer on your GPU’s NVENC encoder, so games keep their frame rate. It works in fullscreen and with the window closed.

  2. The moment

    Press CtrlShiftS

    The buffer becomes a clip. Keep anything from 5 seconds to 10 minutes of history, and change the shortcut whenever you like.

  3. After

    Trim it, mix it, send it

    Cut it down, balance the game against your voice, then stream it straight to a friend’s PC.

Built for the moment right after the play.

Everything runs from a small background recorder. The window only opens when you want to watch, trim or share.

  • 3 tracks

    Every voice on its own track

    Game, microphone and apps like Discord are saved separately. Turn one down while you watch, or mute it.

  • Peer to peer

    Friends without a cloud

    Send an invite link. Clips stream from your PC to theirs, encrypted, and they choose whether to keep a copy.

  • Frame accurate

    Trim before you send

    Drag the edges on a zoomable waveform, loop the part you keep, and the rest is deleted from the file.

  • Tray

    Lives in your tray

    Recording keeps going while the window is closed. Opening Clipture is instant, and closing it frees the memory.

  • 7 themes

    Make it yours

    Graphite, Light, Glitten, Aero, Maid café, Halloween, or your own colours.
