This is the real Clipture app with sample clips. Press Save last 60s, play a clip, or open Friends. Open full screen

Built for the moment right after the play.

Everything runs from a small background recorder. The window only opens when you want to watch, trim or share.

One shortcut, any game

Works in fullscreen games and with the window closed. Change the key and the clip length whenever you like.

Ctrl+Shift+S

Recorded by your GPU

NVIDIA's NVENC encoder does the work, so games keep their frame rate. Keep 5 seconds to 10 minutes of history.

Every voice on its own track
